Mudeungsan UNESCO Global Geopark

2018Certified as a UNESCO Global Geopark

Visit Seoseokdae and Ipseokdae, famous for their columnar joints, the same hexagonal pattern you’ll find on your bojagi.

The rock is volcanic tuff from the Cretaceous period. The highest point is 1,187 m.

Full routes from Jeungsimsa or Wonhyosa to Seoseokdae take about 4.5 to 6 hours.

July heat and rain: stay safe

  • July is hot, humid and often rainy.
  • Start early in the morning.
  • Carry more water than you think you need.
  • Wear hiking shoes. The routes have steep, rocky parts and stairs.
  • Check the forecast. Do not hike in heavy rain, strong wind or lightning.
  • If you feel dizzy or sick, stop, rest in the shade and drink water. Then turn back.
  • Emergency: call 119 for an ambulance, fire or mountain rescue.

Practical Info

Maps

Every stop has an Open map link. In Korea, Naver Map and KakaoMap give the best walking and transit directions.

Public transport

Gwangju Metro Line 1 serves the downtown area, including Culture Complex and Geumnamno 4-ga stations. City buses go to Mudeungsan trailheads such as Jeungsimsa and Wonhyosa. Check your map app for live routes.

Visiting Etiquette

At the cemetery

  • Speak softly and silence your phone.
  • Dress modestly. Follow staff and signs.
  • Do not touch memorial items. Do not eat or play music.
  • Take a quiet moment at the memorial.

Photography

  • Ask before you photograph people, especially families paying respects.
  • Do not pose for casual selfies at graves.
  • Some halls do not allow photos. Check the signs.
  • Do not fly drones near memorial sites.
